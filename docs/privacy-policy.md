# RemindOn 隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日**

本隐私政策适用于 RemindOn Windows 和 macOS 桌面应用，包括 Microsoft Store 版本、普通安装版和便携版。RemindOn 由 Smileher 维护。

## 1. 我们收集什么

RemindOn 不要求注册账号，也不提供广告、用户画像、统计分析或遥测功能。应用不会主动收集或上传你的姓名、邮箱、提醒内容、设置、备份文件或其他个人资料。

## 2. 数据保存在哪里

提醒、提醒文案、计划时间、主题、语言、通知、开机自启和定时操作等设置保存在当前设备的本地应用数据目录：

- Windows：`%USERPROFILE%\.remindon\remindon.json`（v1.2.1 起）
- macOS：`~/Library/Application Support/com.remindon.app/remindon.json`

你可以在应用中主动导入或导出 JSON 备份。导入或导出的文件由你选择位置，并不会自动上传给 RemindOn 维护者。

## 3. 网络连接和更新

普通安装版和便携版可能在启动后每天最多自动检查一次更新，也可以由你在“关于”页面手动检查。更新检查和下载会访问 GitHub Releases；GitHub 连接失败时，应用可能根据你的操作切换到 Gitee Releases。网络请求只用于读取更新清单和下载经过签名或校验的发布文件，不包含提醒内容、应用设置或备份数据。

Microsoft Store 版本由 Microsoft Store 管理安装、更新和卸载。该版本不会使用 RemindOn 的 GitHub/Gitee 更新检查流程。

GitHub、Gitee、Microsoft Store 以及网络服务提供商可能按照各自的隐私政策记录常规网络技术信息，例如 IP 地址、访问时间、请求头和下载记录。RemindOn 不控制这些第三方服务的日志和处理方式。

## 4. 操作系统功能

RemindOn 可以使用操作系统提供的系统托盘、通知、开机自启和电源操作能力。这些功能在你的设备上执行。定时关机、锁定或重启只有在你配置并确认后才会执行，应用不会把这些操作记录或上传给维护者。

## 5. 第三方服务和链接

应用发布、更新和下载可能使用 GitHub、Gitee 或 Microsoft Store。应用和网站也可能提供指向这些服务及项目问题反馈页的链接。访问第三方服务时，请同时阅读其隐私政策；本政策不适用于第三方服务本身。

## 6. 数据删除

你可以在应用中删除提醒和设置，也可以关闭应用后删除上述本地数据文件。导出的备份文件由你自行管理。第三方服务保留的网络日志不由 RemindOn 控制。

## 7. 政策变更和联系

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，维护者会更新本页面并修改生效日期。关于隐私问题，可以在项目的 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Smileher/RemindOn/issues) 页面联系维护者。

# RemindOn Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

This policy applies to the RemindOn desktop application for Windows and macOS, including the Microsoft Store version, the regular installer, and the portable version. RemindOn is maintained by Smileher.

## 1. Information we collect

RemindOn does not require an account and does not provide advertising, profiling, analytics, or telemetry. The app does not intentionally collect or upload your name, email address, reminder content, settings, backup files, or other personal information.

## 2. Where your data is stored

Reminders, reminder text, schedules, theme, language, notification, startup, and scheduled action settings are stored locally in the application data directory on your device:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\.remindon\remindon.json` (from v1.2.1)
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/com.remindon.app/remindon.json`

You can manually import or export a JSON backup in the app. Files you choose to import or export remain under your control and are not automatically uploaded to the RemindOn maintainer.

## 3. Network connections and updates

The regular installer and portable version may check for updates at most once per day after startup. You can also check manually from the About page. Update checks and downloads use GitHub Releases; if GitHub cannot be reached, the app may switch to Gitee Releases when you choose that option or when the fallback countdown completes. These requests are only used to read update metadata and download signed or checksum-verified release files. They do not include your reminders, settings, or backup data.

The Microsoft Store version uses Microsoft Store to manage installation, updates, and removal. It does not use RemindOn's GitHub/Gitee update flow.

GitHub, Gitee, Microsoft Store, and network service providers may process ordinary technical connection information under their own policies, such as IP address, request time, request headers, and download records. RemindOn does not control those third-party logs or processing.

## 4. Operating system features

RemindOn may use operating system features for the system tray, notifications, startup, and power actions. These features run on your device. Shutdown, lock, or restart actions occur only after you configure and confirm them; RemindOn does not send records of those actions to the maintainer.

## 5. Third-party services and links

Publishing, updating, and downloading the app may use GitHub, Gitee, or Microsoft Store. The app and website may also link to those services and the project issue tracker. When you visit a third-party service, read its privacy policy as well; this policy does not govern the third party itself.

## 6. Deleting data

You can delete reminders and settings in the app, or close the app and remove the local data file described above. You control exported backup files. RemindOn does not control how long third-party services retain network logs.

## 7. Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and its effective date will be changed. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the project's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Smileher/RemindOn/issues) page.
